Routines for Living AloneGamified self-care

Type
Graduate team project (3)
Role
UX research, screen design
Year
2022.09 – 2022.12

We started from the insight that young adults living alone feel today's hassle before tomorrow's benefit. Three rounds of progressive one-on-one interviews shaped our personas, and we built a prototype that adds missions, team clubs and feedback to the cue, craving, response and reward habit loop. We later entered it in a campus startup competition.
