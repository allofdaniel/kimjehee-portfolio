Modoo Startup, 2nd cohort · ongoing

In 2026 I advanced to round one of the second Modoo Startup cohort, a Korean government startup program, on the general and technology track. I am working through one-on-one mentoring with a mentor institution, and the round-two selection review is scheduled for December.

1. Define the brand

The name is Ongo (溫故), ‘warming up the old’. The line is ‘wishing through pictures, the way we live now’.

Korean folk paintings such as peonies and moon jars go on baby blankets, each with a story card explaining what the painting wishes for.

The first gift-givers I am designing for are people in their twenties and thirties choosing a stylish gift for friends, more than grandparents.

2. Zodiac drafts

A collection that makes the baby's birth-year animal its first picture.

I drafted a baby horse between a red sun and blue hills for 2026, a gentle lamb beside a moon jar for 2027, and the two together.

The drafts are AI-generated and may differ from the final product.

Zodiac collection page ↗

3. Gift survey

A two-minute survey, run before production, on how people actually choose baby gifts. It does not ask for names.

It asks how many birth, 100-day and first-birthday gifts people gave in the past year, who and what the latest one was for, what they bought, how much they spent, where, why they chose it, what was frustrating, and what gifts are coming up.

Respondents can leave contact details for a launch notice if they wish.
