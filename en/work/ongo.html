Ongo 溫故Baby blankets with Korean folk painting

Type
My own brand
Role
Founder · brand planning, design
Channel
@ongo.won_archive
Year
2026 –

Ongo (溫故) puts Korean folk painting, such as peonies and moon jars, on baby blankets. Each comes with a story card explaining what the painting wishes for, so a baby's first blanket carries an old way of making a wish.

I made draft designs for a zodiac collection that uses the baby's birth-year animal as its first picture, and I run a two-minute survey on how people actually choose baby gifts before production.
