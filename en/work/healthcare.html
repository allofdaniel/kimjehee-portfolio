School Posture ScreeningAdapting a body-analysis device for schools

Company
Healthcare & robotics company
Role
Service planning, specs, screen design (solo)
Year
2024

I led, on my own, the adaptation of a joint-tracking posture analysis device for elementary schools. The goals: screen a whole class in one continuous session, cut a long report down to one or two pages, and send each student's follow-up care via KakaoTalk.

I sorted the business team's requests by priority, sized the work and development effort with an IA, then wrote the screen designs and functional specs for both the screening program and the admin web, staying with the project through design, development and testing.

  2. IA & effort sizing
  3. Screening program
  4. Admin web
